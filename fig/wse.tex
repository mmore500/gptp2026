\caption{
\textbf{Accelerator-based HPC Case Study.}
\footnotesize
Wafer-Scale Engine (WSE) architecture consists of independently-executing processing elements (PEs), arranged in a grid lattice (panel \ref{moreno:fig:wse:architecture}).
To accomodate constraints in host-device communication and on-device memory (48kB), observation of lineage histories was conducted using a best-effort approach --- making use of structured markers to estimate relatedness between sparsely sampled genomes (panel \ref{moreno:fig:wse:phylogeny}).
Such a strategy gracefully tolerates missing data, such as a localized corruption fault in device-to-host transfer arising in one piece of WSE-3 hardware (panel \ref{moreno:fig:wse:fault}).
Best-effort relaxation is extended to migration between per-PE subpopulations, which are conducted asynchronously.
Before correction, a small directional bias in migration detectably influenced long-term lineage histories (panel \ref{moreno:fig:wse:bias}).
In experiments exploring the evolution of hypermutator traits, where expected duration was not known \textit{a priori}, time series records of local hypermutator prevalence were curated on a best-effort within a fixed per-PE memory budget using a generalized ring-buffer stragegy (panel \ref{moreno:fig:wse:timeseries}; video animation at \url{https://hopth.ru/ej}).
}
