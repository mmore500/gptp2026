\section{Conclusion} \label{moreno:sec:conclusion}

\begin{itemize}
\item best-effort approaches are promising, as we have shown...
\item digital evolution is uniquely well-suited for best-effort computing
\begin{itemize}
  \item chaotic/stochastic and therefore robust to perturbations (extinctions, irregular migration patterns, etc.)
  \item also, unless huge effort is invested to ensure bit-leel rperoducibility, meawningful identical outcomes are dubiouse at scale
  \item rich methods already developed in biology to develop with stochastic, incomplete data
  \item in the case of artificial life and evolutionary computation, there is also greater epistemological flexibility
\end{itemize}
\item pre-existing work has already incorporated \textit{ad hoc} best-effort computing approaches into digital evolution (cites)
\begin{itemize}
   \item particularly notable work, in the realm of artificial life includes Tom Ray and Dave Ackley
   \item as has been pointed out by Dave Ackley, artificial life has a role to play in exploring and pushing the boundaries of post-deterministic computing
   \item as with most existing studies of best-effort computing, however, attention has focused on bespoke hardware
   \item by translating these approaches into mainstream high-performance computing writ large, digital evolution may play a role in laying a principled, rigorous, and generalizable foundation for broader development of post-determinstic computing writ large
\end{itemize}
